# Cool it, power it

A one-page planner for homes and small hotels:

- AC sizing room by room (Manual J-style), split vs central vs VRF
- Yearly electricity use, in kWh and money, from the house or from an appliance list
- Best number of solar panels and battery size (25-year value), with local prices and tariffs
  (Vietnam: EVN household steps and business time-of-day prices)
- 3D building with sun, roof and hour-by-hour power flow
- Existing building from the customer's bills (amount or kWh) and a few roof facts, or a full new-building plan
- Customer projects saved on the device, export/import between devices
- Company profile, equipment and price list; itemised proposal in English or Vietnamese with VAT, cash flow, IRR, loan option, warranty and signatures
- Checks for odd inputs and a confidence label

Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Estimates, not a quote.

Live: https://tnguyenfr1.github.io/sun-cool-sizer/
